## Classic and Unlimited Wordle

One-board word guessing and repeat-play versions.

- **[Wordle](https://www.nytimes.com/games/wordle/index.html)** — The original daily five-letter guessing game from The New York Times. _(Daily)_
- **[hello wordl](https://hellowordl.net/)** — Play Wordle-style puzzles with adjustable word lengths and repeat rounds. _(Unlimited)_
- **[Word Master](https://octokatherine.github.io/word-master/)** — An open-source Wordle-style game for playing additional rounds. _(Unlimited)_
- **[Wordle Infinite](https://wordles.org/)** — Keep playing new word-guessing puzzles without a daily limit. _(Unlimited)_
- **[Wordle Game](https://wordlegame.org/)** — A browser-based five-letter word game with daily and unlimited options. _(Daily · Unlimited)_
- **[Word500](https://word500.com/)** — A tougher word-deduction challenge with daily and practice play. _(Daily · Practice)_

## Rule Twists and Themed Wordle

Familiar colored clues with an extra rule, theme, or objective.

- **[Absurdle](https://qntm.org/files/absurdle/absurdle.html)** — An adversarial Wordle variant that avoids committing to an answer for as long as possible. _(Unlimited)_
- **[Antiwordle](https://antiwordle.com/)** — Reverse the usual goal: avoid solving the hidden word for as long as you can.
- **[Fibble](https://fibble.xyz/)** — A Wordle-like game with misleading clues that must be reasoned through.
- **[Phrazle](https://phrazle.org/)** — Guess a multi-word phrase using Wordle-style letter feedback. _(Daily · Unlimited)_
- **[Phoodle](https://www.phoodle.net/)** — A daily five-letter word puzzle themed around food and cooking. _(Daily)_

## Multi-Board Wordle

Two, four, eight, or many hidden words solved with shared guesses.

- **[Dordle](https://zaratustra.itch.io/dordle)** — Solve two hidden words at the same time with shared guesses. _(Daily)_
- **[Quordle](https://www.merriam-webster.com/games/quordle/)** — Solve four five-letter words simultaneously on the official Merriam-Webster game. _(Daily · Practice)_
- **[Octordle](https://www.merriam-webster.com/games/octordle/)** — Eight Wordle boards share your guesses in this multi-word challenge. _(Daily)_
- **[Hexordle](https://hexordle.com/)** — A six-board Wordle-style puzzle for players who like multi-grid deduction. _(Daily)_
- **[Sedecordle](https://sedecordle.com/)** — Solve sixteen words with one common set of guesses. _(Daily · Practice)_
- **[Duotrigordle](https://duotrigordle.com/)** — A thirty-two-board Wordle variant built around shared guesses. _(Daily)_
- **[Polydle](https://polydle.github.io/)** — Choose how many Wordle boards to solve, with multiple challenge styles. _(Daily · Unlimited)_
- **[Kilordle](https://www.kilordle.net/)** — Take the multi-board idea to the extreme with one thousand hidden words.

## About This List

- **Direct game links:** Links point to the game or its publisher whenever possible, rather than answer sites or ad-heavy mirrors.
- **Curated, not exhaustive:** This is a growing collection. Availability, game modes, and free-play limits can change.
- **Different formats:** Not every listed game uses Wordle's exact letter-feedback mechanic. The *Related Daily Word Puzzles* section intentionally includes adjacent daily puzzle formats.
- **No spoilers:** This list does not publish daily answers.

Found an outdated URL, missing game, or incorrect description? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/buainoai/awesome-wordle-games/issues) or read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to suggest a change.
